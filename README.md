# Mahachi Projects

Marketing website for **Mahachi Projects** — a 100% black-owned building, construction &
maintenance company in Johannesburg (a division of Sailoni Mbewe Investment Holdings (Pty) Ltd).

The site is a single-page, mobile-friendly landing page built on static HTML/CSS/JS.
`index.html` is the main page; `contact.html`, `agent.html`, `blog.html` and `properties.html`
redirect to the relevant section of the homepage.

## ⚠️ Two things YOU must do so leads actually arrive

1. **Activate the contact form (one-time).**
   The "Get In Touch" form uses [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co) to email submissions to
   `admin@mahachiprojects.co.za` — no server needed. The **first** time someone submits the form,
   FormSubmit sends a confirmation email to that inbox. **Click the link in it once** to switch the
   form on. After that, every enquiry lands in your inbox automatically.

2. **Turn on visitor tracking (optional but recommended).**
   In `index.html`, find the `GOOGLE ANALYTICS (GA4)` comment block in the `<head>`, remove the
   surrounding `<!-- ... -->`, and replace `G-XXXXXXXXXX` with your free GA4 Measurement ID from
   <https://analytics.google.com>. This lets you see how many people visit and where they come from.

## How leads reach you today

- **"Get a Free Quote" buttons** open a popup that sends the details straight to your
  **WhatsApp** (`+27 60 334 1133`) — the fastest, most reliable channel.
- **Floating WhatsApp button** (bottom-right) and header/footer WhatsApp links.
- **Contact form** emails you via FormSubmit (see step 1 above).
- **Click-to-call** phone numbers work on mobile.

## Get found on Google (do these once)

- Create a free **Google Business Profile** for Mahachi Projects (address, phone, photos, hours).
- Add the site to **Google Search Console** and submit `sitemap.xml`.
- Ask happy customers to leave Google reviews.
